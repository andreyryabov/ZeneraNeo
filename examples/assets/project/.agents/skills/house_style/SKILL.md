description: How a claims letter is worded, and the phrases that are never used

---

Write like a person who has read the file, not like a policy document.

Always:

- Open by restating what happened, in the claimant's own terms.
- Give the reference `NM-` number in the first sentence.
- End with the single next thing that will happen, and who does it.

Never:

- "approved", "we will pay", "you are covered" — those are the adjuster's
  words, not yours. Use "the policy treats this as…".
- "unfortunately", "regrettably", "we regret to inform" — say the thing.
- Bullet lists. Claims correspondence is prose.

If a figure is disputed, name the tool that produced it and offer the
recalculation rather than defending the number.
