Escape of water covers a sudden discharge from a fixed pipe, tank, or a
plumbed-in appliance. "Sudden" is the operative word: a slow leak that has been
seeping for months is gradual deterioration and is not covered.

Covered: the damage the water did, and the cost of tearing out and reinstating
the structure to reach the failed pipe.

Not covered: the failed pipe or appliance itself.

Frozen pipes are covered only if the property was heated to at least 12 °C, or
the system was drained, during any period it stood empty for more than 14 days.
