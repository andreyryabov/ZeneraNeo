# Meetings at Northwind

## Rooms and links

Every meeting has a Tempo Meet link of the form `https://meet.northwind.io/<team>-<topic>`,
for example `https://meet.northwind.io/eng-platform-sync`. In Berlin the bookable rooms are
**Spree** (8 people, screen), **Havel** (4 people) and **Tegel** (16 people, for all-hands).
Put the room in the event's `location` as `Berlin HQ · Spree` and the link in the description.

## Lengths

| Kind                 | Length     |
| -------------------- | ---------- |
| 1:1                  | 30 minutes |
| Team sync            | 30 minutes |
| Readiness / go-no-go | 45 minutes |
| Incident review      | 60 minutes |
| All-hands            | 50 minutes |

## Focus time

Engineers block **Deep work** from 13:00 to 15:00 Berlin time on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
Nobody books meetings over it except for incidents.
